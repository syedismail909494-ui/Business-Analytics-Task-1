# Week 11 – Business Analysis Using Bubble Chart

## 📊 Tableau Public Dashboard

🔗 [View Tableau Public Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/authoring/TASK11_17910934634500/BusinessAnalysisDashboard#1)

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyze and compare different business categories using a Bubble Chart and supporting visualizations in Tableau Public.

The dashboard helps identify the most and least significant business categories based on sales, profit, regional performance, and sales trends.

---

## 📝 Problem Statement

The objective of this task is to analyze business performance using categorical and numerical fields such as **Category, Sales, Profit, Order Date, and Region**.

A Bubble Chart is used as the main visualization to compare sales across different business categories.

---

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies Used

* Tableau Public
* Superstore Dataset
* Microsoft Excel

---

## 📊 Visualizations Created

### 1. 🫧 Bubble Chart – Sales by Category

* Displays sales performance across different categories.
* Bubble size represents Sales.
* Different colors represent different categories.
* Helps identify categories with higher and lower sales.

### 2. 📊 Profit by Category

* A bar chart is used to compare profit across categories.
* Helps identify the categories contributing more to profitability.

### 3. 🥧 Sales by Region

* A pie chart shows the distribution of sales across different regions.
* Helps compare regional business performance.

### 4. 📈 Sales Trend

* A line chart displays sales performance over time.
* Helps identify changes and trends in business sales.

### 5. 🔽 Region Filter

* An interactive Region filter is included in the dashboard.
* Users can select different regions to analyze their business performance.

---

## 💡 5 Business Insights

1. **Technology** is one of the leading categories in terms of sales, indicating strong customer demand.

2. **Office Supplies** makes a significant contribution to overall sales and remains an important business category.

3. **Furniture** contributes to total sales, but its profitability should also be considered when evaluating its overall performance.

4. Sales performance differs across **regions**, indicating that regional factors can influence business results.

5. The **Sales Trend** helps identify changes in sales over time and highlights periods of higher and lower business activity.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

### Recommendation 1

Focus on high-performing categories and regions by maintaining product availability and using targeted marketing strategies.

### Recommendation 2

Improve lower-performing categories by reviewing pricing, promotions, product selection, and customer demand to increase sales and profitability.

---

## 📌 Key Findings

The Bubble Chart provides an easy visual comparison of sales across business categories. Larger bubbles indicate higher sales, while smaller bubbles indicate comparatively lower sales.

The combination of the Bubble Chart, Profit by Category, Sales by Region, and Sales Trend provides a broader understanding of overall business performance.
