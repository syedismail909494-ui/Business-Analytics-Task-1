## Business Questions & Answers

### 1. Which customer segment generates the highest sales?

**Answer:** Member customers generate the highest sales across all cities.

### 2. Which customer group purchases most frequently?

**Answer:** Member customers purchase more frequently than Normal customers.

### 3. Which payment method is most preferred?

**Answer:** Cash is the most preferred payment method, followed by E-wallet and Credit Card.

### 4. Which product category is the most popular?

**Answer:** Food and Beverages is the most popular product category.

### 5. Which city contributes the highest revenue?

**Answer:** Naypyitaw contributes the highest revenue.

### 6. Which customer segment should the business focus on?

**Answer:** Member customers should be the primary target for future marketing campaigns.

##  Business Insights

- Member customers contribute the highest overall sales.
- Cash is the most frequently used payment method.
- Food and Beverages generates the highest sales among all product categories.
- Naypyitaw is the highest revenue-generating city.
- Branch performance is balanced, with Giza showing a slight lead in sales and quantity.

## Recommendations

- Strengthen loyalty programs to retain Member customers.
- Increase inventory and promotions for Food and Beverages products.
- Promote digital payment methods through cashback and special offers.
