## Business Insights
## 1.The Tree Map clearly identifies the categories and sub-categories that contribute the most to overall business performance.
## 2.High-performing sub-categories occupy larger areas in the Tree Map, indicating their greater contribution to sales.
## 3.The analysis shows that some categories perform better than others and therefore have a greater impact on overall revenue.
## 4.Sales performance varies across different time periods, helping identify periods of higher and lower business activity.
## 5.Geographical analysis highlights differences in business performance across locations, helping identify stronger and weaker markets.

## Business Recommendations
## 1.Focus on high-performing categories: Increase inventory, marketing, and promotional activities for categories and sub-categories that generate strong sales and profitability.
## 2.Improve underperforming categories: Analyze pricing, discounts, demand, and customer preferences for weaker categories and develop strategies to improve their performance.

## Conclusion

## The Tableau dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of business performance across different categories and sub-categories. The Tree Map makes it easy to identify the areas that contribute most significantly to sales, while the supporting visualizations provide additional insights into trends, product performance, and geographical distribution.
## Overall, the analysis demonstrates how Tableau can transform business data into meaningful visual insights that help organizations identify opportunities, improve resource allocation, and make better data-driven business decisions.
