## Business Insights:

## 1. Monthly Sales Trend:
### Total sales recorded are approximately 12.64 million.
### Sales show fluctuations throughout the year, with noticeable declines in February, April, July, and October.
### Sales increase strongly from August to September and reach their highest levels in November and December.
### December records the highest monthly sales at approximately 1.58 million.
### The upward trend toward the end of the year indicates stronger business performance during the final months.

## 2. Sales by Category:
### Technology is the highest-performing category with sales of approximately 4.74 million.
### Furniture contributes around 4.11 million.
### Office Supplies contributes approximately 3.79 million.
### Technology contributes the largest share of overall category sales, showing strong demand for technology-related products.

## 3. Sales by Region:
### Sales are distributed across multiple regions, with some regions contributing significantly more than others.
### The dashboard shows noticeable differences in regional sales performance.
### Regions with higher sales can be studied further to identify successful products, customers, and sales strategies.

## 4. Sub-Category Performance:
### Bookcases show one of the highest sales values among the visible sub-categories, at approximately 1.47 million.
### Appliances also contribute strongly, with approximately 1.01 million in sales.
### Other sub-categories such as Accessories and Art contribute comparatively lower sales.
### This indicates that certain product sub-categories have a greater impact on overall revenue.

## 5. Overall Business Trend:
### The business demonstrates an overall positive sales trend toward the end of the year.
### The strong performance during November and December could be associated with increased customer purchasing activity and seasonal demand.

## Business Recommendations:

### Focus on the Technology Category Since Technology generates the highest category sales, the business can maintain sufficient inventory and introduce targeted promotions for high-performing technology products.
### Analyze and Improve Low-Sales Periods Sales decline during months such as February, April, July, and October. Special discounts, promotional campaigns, and customer offers can be planned during these periods to improve sales.
### Increase Inventory Before Peak Months Since November and December show the strongest sales, inventory and marketing activities should be planned in advance to take advantage of the increased demand.
### Improve Lower-Performing Regions Regional sales differences should be investigated to identify why some regions generate lower revenue. Localized promotions and improved product availability can be considered.
### Promote High-Performing Sub-Categories Products from strong sub-categories such as Bookcases and Appliances can receive additional marketing attention, while lower-performing sub-categories can be reviewed for pricing, demand, and product strategy.

## Conclusion

### The Tableau dashboard provides a clear overview of business sales performance across time, category, sub-category, and region. The analysis shows that Technology is the leading category, while sales generally become stronger toward the end of the year. November and December are the strongest sales periods, whereas several months during the middle and early part of the year show fluctuations. The business can use these insights to improve inventory planning, strengthen high-performing categories, target weaker regions, and introduce promotional strategies during low-sales periods.
