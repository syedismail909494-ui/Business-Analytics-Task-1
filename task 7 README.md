# Supermarket Sales Analysis Dashboard Using Tableau

## Business Insights
### 1. Food and Beverages is the Top-Performing Product Line

Food and beverages generated the highest sales at approximately 56,145, making it the strongest product category in the business.

- Insight: The company should maintain sufficient inventory and promotional activities for this product line because it contributes significantly to overall revenue.

### 2. Health and Beauty Has the Lowest Sales

Health and beauty recorded the lowest sales at approximately 49,194.

- Insight: This category may require additional promotions, discounts, better product positioning, or customer-targeted marketing to improve its performance.

### 3. Sales Show a Positive Relationship with Quantity

The scatter plot indicates a positive relationship between quantity sold and sales. Generally, higher quantities are associated with higher sales values.

- Insight: Increasing product volume through promotions, bundles, and quantity-based offers could help increase revenue.

### 4. January Recorded the Highest Monthly Sales

Monthly sales were:

Month	Sales
- January 2019	- 116,292
- March 2019	  - 109,456
- February 2019	- 97,219

January generated the highest sales, while February recorded the lowest.

- Insight: The business should investigate what caused the strong January performance and apply similar strategies during weaker months.

### 5. Naypyitaw Is a Strong Revenue-Generating City

The dashboard shows Naypyitaw generating approximately 110,568.71 in sales, making it one of the strongest-performing cities.

- Insight: The company can focus marketing campaigns and inventory availability in high-performing cities while developing strategies to improve weaker locations.

## Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides a clear overview of sales performance across products, cities, quantities, and months. The analysis shows that Food and beverages is the leading product line, while Health and beauty has the lowest sales. January achieved the highest monthly sales, and the scatter plot indicates that increasing quantity sold is generally associated with higher sales.

Overall, the business has a balanced product portfolio, but there are opportunities to improve weaker product categories and months. By focusing on high-performing products and cities, improving underperforming categories, and using quantity-based promotions, the company can increase revenue and improve overall business performance.
