# HR Analytics – Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using Tableau Public. The dashboard helps understand why employees leave an organization by examining factors such as department, business travel, age group, and distance from home.

The main goal is to identify employee groups with higher attrition rates and provide insights that can help organizations improve employee retention.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Tableau Public
- Microsoft Excel / CSV Dataset
- GitHub

## 📁 Dataset Details

The HR Analytics dataset contains employee information and attrition details.

- **Total Employees:** 1,470
- **Employees Who Left:** 237
- **Employees Who Stayed:** 1,233
- **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.12%

### Key Fields
- Employee Number
- Age
- Department
- Business Travel
- Distance From Home
- Attrition
- Employee Count

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes four visualizations:

1. **Attrition by Department** – Compares employee attrition across different departments.
2. **Attrition by Business Travel** – Analyzes attrition based on employees' business travel frequency.
3. **Employee Attrition by Age Group** – Identifies age groups with higher employee attrition.
4. **Attrition by Distance from Home** – Examines attrition across different distance groups.

## 🔍 Key Insights

1. The overall employee attrition rate is approximately **16.12%**.
2. The **Sales department** has the highest attrition rate at approximately **20.63%**.
3. Employees who travel frequently for business have a higher attrition rate of approximately **24.91%**.
4. Employees aged **18–25** have the highest attrition rate among the age groups, at approximately **35.77%**.
5. Employees living 11–15 miles from work have an attrition rate of approximately **21.74%**.
6. The findings highlight employee age, department, business travel, and distance from home as useful factors to investigate when studying attrition.

## 💡 Recommendations

1. **Improve Employee Retention:** Develop suitable retention strategies for departments with higher attrition.
2. **Support Frequent Travellers:** Review travel schedules and provide better support for employees who travel frequently.
3. **Support Younger Employees:** Introduce mentoring, career development, and training opportunities for younger employees.
4. **Review Work-Life Balance:** Examine whether travel requirements and commuting distance affect employee satisfaction.
5. **Monitor Attrition Regularly:** Use dashboards to track attrition patterns and support data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how Tableau can be used to visualize HR data, identify employee attrition patterns, and communicate insights that support better workforce planning and employee retention.
