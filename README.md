Business Insights:

1. Top-rated cuisines receive consistently higher customer satisfaction, indicating that these cuisine types are more popular and have better customer experiences.

2. The restaurant market is concentrated around a few cuisine types, as shown by the donut chart, while several other cuisines have relatively fewer restaurants.

3. Higher prices do not always result in higher customer ratings. Some affordable restaurants receive ratings that are equal to or better than expensive restaurants.

4. Certain cuisines provide excellent value for money, achieving high average ratings even at lower price ranges. These cuisines are attractive to budget-conscious customers.

5. Restaurant owners can use customer ratings to improve business performance. Cuisines with lower average ratings may need improvements in food quality, service, or customer experience to become more competitive.
