🎯 Objective

The objective of this task is to analyze business performance across different categories using a Heat Map and other visualizations in Tableau Public.

📌 Visualizations Created

1. Business Performance Heat Map
   
   - Shows business performance across different categories.
   - Uses color intensity to compare values easily.

2. Category-wise Analysis
   
   - Compares business performance across different categories.

3. Regional Analysis
   
   - Shows differences in business performance across regions.

4. Additional Business Visualization
   
   - Provides further comparison and analysis of the selected business measures.

🔍 Interactive Filter

- Category/Region Filter
- Allows users to select specific categories or regions and interactively analyze the dashboard.

💡 Business Insights

1. Business performance varies across different categories.
2. Some categories show stronger performance compared to others.
3. Regional performance differs across the selected business measures.
4. The Heat Map makes it easy to identify high and low-performing areas.
5. The dashboard helps compare business performance across multiple dimensions.

📈 Business Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing categories and regions to maintain business performance.
2. Analyze low-performing categories and regions to identify opportunities for improvement.
