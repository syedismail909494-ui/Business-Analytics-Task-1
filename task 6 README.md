# Sales and Profit Trends Over Time

### https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/syed.ismail4612/viz/week6_17879396572330/Dashboard1?publish=yes

## Insights:
### 1.Monthly Sales Trend:
The Area Chart shows fluctuations in sales over time, with certain periods experiencing noticeable increases in sales. This indicates that customer demand changes across different months.
### 2.Sales by Category:
The category-wise analysis helps identify which product categories contribute the most to overall sales. Higher-performing categories can be given more attention in future sales and marketing strategies.
### 3.Profit by Region:
Regional analysis shows that profitability varies across different regions. This helps the business identify strong-performing regions and areas where performance may need improvement.
### 4.Sales by Sub-Category:
Some sub-categories contribute significantly more to sales than others. These high-performing products can be prioritized for inventory management and promotional activities.

## Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides a clear overview of the Superstore's business performance by analyzing sales and profit across time, product categories, sub-categories, and regions. The Area Chart highlights changes in monthly sales and makes it easier to identify periods of growth and decline.

The analysis shows that business performance varies over time and across different product and regional segments. Identifying high-performing categories, sub-categories, and regions can help the business focus its resources more effectively. The insights from the dashboard can support better decisions related to sales planning, inventory management, marketing strategies, and regional performance improvement.

Overall, Tableau provides an effective way to transform business data into meaningful visual insights and supports data-driven decision-making.
