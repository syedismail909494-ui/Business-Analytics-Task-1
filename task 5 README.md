## Business Insights
- Box office collections vary significantly across movies, showing that different movie titles have very different financial performances.
- Several movies have high box office values close to 1 billion, indicating strong commercial success.
- Some movies have much lower box office collections, showing that not every movie performs equally well in the market.
- The median box office collection differs between movie titles, which helps compare the typical financial performance of each movie.
- The box plot shows a wide distribution of collections for several titles, suggesting that movie revenue can vary considerably and may be influenced by factors such as budget, audience interest, marketing, and production quality.

## Conclusion

- This analysis used a Box Plot in Tableau to examine the distribution of box office collections across different movie titles. The visualization clearly shows the minimum, maximum, median, and overall variation in box office performance. The results indicate that movie revenue varies significantly, with some movies achieving very high collections while others generate comparatively lower revenue.
- Overall, the Box Plot provides a simple and effective way to identify differences in business performance and understand the distribution of box office collections. This analysis can help movie businesses identify successful revenue patterns and support better decision-making for future movie production and investment.
